Device-Level Data Controls

You can reset or remove advertising identifiers directly on your device:

  • Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID
  • Settings → Apps → [Game] → Storage → Clear Data
Data Retention

Firebase and AdMob automatically handle retention and deletion of analytics and advertising data according to Google’s policies.

Where applicable, we may instruct third-party providers to process deletion requests within up to 30 days.
