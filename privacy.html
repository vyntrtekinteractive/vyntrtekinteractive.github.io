Privacy Policy (Google Play)

Vyntrtek Interactive ("we," "our," or "us") operates mobile applications and hyper-casual games built primarily using the GDevelop engine. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you play our games or interact with our software services.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or require personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise physical location.

However, our third-party service providers (Firebase and Google AdMob) may collect certain non-personally identifiable technical information automatically to ensure game performance and serve advertisements.

A. Automated Technical & Analytics Data (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics) to understand game performance, fix bugs, and improve user experience. The data collected may include:

  • Device model, operating system version, and screen resolution.
  • Gameplay metrics (e.g., session length, levels completed, score events).
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics.

B. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display ads within our free games. AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising ID (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID).
  • IP address (used to infer general coarse location such as country or city).
  • Interaction with displayed ads (clicks, views, and impressions).

2. How We Use Information

  • Ensure smooth technical performance and fix crashes across devices.
  • Deliver non-intrusive, relevant advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Monitor gameplay engagement to refine game mechanics and level design.

3. Third-Party Services & Links

Our games integrate trusted third-party SDKs that operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services / Firebase: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

4. Children’s Privacy

Our games are designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).

If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Data Choice

  • Android users may opt out of personalized ads:
    Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization / Reset advertising ID
  • Since we do not store personal profiles, data requests are limited to third-party identifiers handled by SDK providers.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date.
